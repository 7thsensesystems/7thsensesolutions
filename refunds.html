Refund Policy

Effective November 24, 2026

I want you to feel good about working with me. This policy explains how refunds work for each type of purchase. If something isn't right, email me first. Most issues can be fixed with a quick conversation.

Quick wins and fixed-scope projects

  • Before work starts: if you cancel before I've started, you'll get a full refund, minus any non-refundable payment processing fees.
  • After work starts: payments for work already completed aren't refundable. If you cancel partway through, I'll refund the portion of the fee for work not yet started.
  • If I can't deliver what we scoped, I'll make it right or refund the undelivered portion. Your call.

Systems builds and ongoing work

Larger projects and ongoing support follow the payment and cancellation terms in your proposal or agreement. Those terms are always agreed on in writing before work begins.

Digital products

Because digital products are delivered instantly and can't be returned, all sales are final. If a file is broken, missing, or not what was described, email me within 14 days of purchase and I'll fix it, replace it, or refund you.

Free resources and the newsletter

Free resources and newsletter signups don't involve payment, so there's nothing to refund. You can unsubscribe anytime.

How to request a refund

Email jordan@7thsensesolutions.com with your name, what you purchased, and the date. Approved refunds go back to your original payment method, usually within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank.

Questions? Email jordan@7thsensesolutions.com and I'll get back to you within two business days.
