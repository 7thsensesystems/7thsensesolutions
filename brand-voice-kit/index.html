  1. 1

    Talk to the AI

    About 20 minutes

    Get ready

    Find 5 to 10 things you wrote yourself: old texts to customers, emails, Facebook or Instagram captions. Real and messy is better than polished.

    Then

    1. Open the Claude or ChatGPT app and start a new chat.
    2. Tap Copy prompt below, paste it into the chat, and send it.
    3. Tap the voice button. Answer each question out loud.
    4. When it asks for your writing, leave voice mode and paste it all in.
    5. It writes your voice guide. If anything sounds off, tell it.

    If voice mode stops early, no problem. Finish by typing in the same chat.

    I own a small business. Interview me so you can learn how I talk. Then write my voice guide so AI can write messages that sound like me.

I'll answer out loud. Ask ONE short question at a time and wait for me. Keep it casual. No compliments. If my answer is short, ask one quick follow-up.

The questions:
1. What do you sell, and who are your regulars?
2. When someone walks in, what do you say to them?
3. If your shop were a person, how would they talk?
4. What words or nicknames do you say all the time?
5. What words make you cringe in ads?
6. Do you use emoji, exclamation points, or all caps? How much?
7. How do you talk about prices and deals?
8. How do you want customers to feel when they read your messages?

After question 8, ask me to paste 5 to 10 things I wrote. Study how I really write: sentence length, how I start and end, punctuation, emoji, words I repeat. If my writing and my answers don't match, go with my writing.

Then write my voice guide in this exact layout. Use my own words where you can. No em dashes.

MY VOICE GUIDE
Who we are: (1 sentence)
How we sound: (3 short phrases, each with one line on what it means)
Words we use: (list)
Words we never use: (list)
How we write: (sentence length, openers, sign-off, emoji, exclamation points)
Before and after: (2 examples. BEFORE sounds like generic AI. AFTER sounds like me.)
Rules: (5 short rules)

Then ask me what feels off and fix it. Start with question 1.
  2. 2

    Save your voice guide

    2 minutes

    When the AI hands you your voice guide, copy the whole thing and save it somewhere easy, like your phone's notes app. You'll paste it in Step 3.

    See an exampleA finished guide for a made-up bakery 
    MY VOICE GUIDE

Who we are: A small family bakery on Hollis Street. Neighbors, office folks, and parents after school pickup.

How we sound:
- Like a neighbor behind the counter. We use first names and talk like we'll see you tomorrow.
- Short and specific. We name the item, the time, and the price.
- A little dry humor. One joke, max. Usually about how fast things sell out.

Words we use: swing by, the good bread, while they last, see you Saturday

Words we never use: indulge, decadent, elevate, artisanal, treat yourself, don't miss out

How we write: Short sentences. Open with the news. One exclamation point at most. One emoji or none. Sign off with "See you soon. Dana"

Before and after:
BEFORE: Indulge in our decadent pumpkin pies! Perfect for family gatherings and cozy nights. Order now before they're gone!
AFTER: Pumpkin pie is back. $22 whole, $4 a slice. Preorders open now for Thanksgiving week.

BEFORE: We're thrilled to announce our brand-new holiday cookie collection, crafted with love.
AFTER: Holiday cookie boxes start Friday. A dozen mixed, $18. Last year they were gone by noon.

Rules:
- Lead with what's new.
- Name the item, the price, and the time.
- Say it once. No "hurry" unless it's true.
- No fancy words.
- If I wouldn't say it at the register, cut it.

Does it sound like a robot?

    
Ask yourself: would I say this out loud at the counter? If not, fix it.

    
    
      
  • Punctuation you never typeLong dashes, semicolons. Cut them.
    • 
      
  • "It's not just a donut, it's a tradition."Just say the point.
    • 
      
  • Everything in threes"Fresh, fluffy, and unforgettable." Use what's true. Often that's one thing.
    • 
      
  • Canned openers"Calling all..." "We're thrilled to announce..." Start with the news.
    • 
      
  • Fancy wordsIndulge, elevate, decadent. If a car wash could use the word, cut it.
    • 
      
  • Vague words"Mouthwatering flavors." "Cozy vibes." Name the real thing: the flavor, the item, the price.
    • 
      
  • Decoration emojiOne at the end of every line, sparkles everywhere. Use emoji the way you really do, or skip them.
    • 
      
  • Saying it twiceThe last line repeats the first. Delete it.
    • 
      
  • Fake hurry"Last chance!" when it isn't.
    • 
    

    
AI apps change, and so do their habits. If something new sounds off, add it to the robot list in your Project instructions.
